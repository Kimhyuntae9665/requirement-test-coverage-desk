# Enterprise chronology and limits

This project studies requirement/test traceability as an original synthetic portfolio implementation. It uses no Volkswagen data, private architecture, Codebeamer APIs, or production ALM integration.

| Date | Primary-source statement | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 Dec 2024 | [PTC, Microsoft and Volkswagen development partnership](https://investor.ptc.com/resources/news/news-details/2024/PTC-Partners-with-Microsoft-and-Volkswagen-Group-to-Develop-Codebeamer-Generative-AI-Copilot/default.aspx) announced plans to develop a Codebeamer generative AI copilot and expected selected-customer beta in early 2025. | A delivered general-release capability or independently measured benefit. |
| 22 May 2025 | [Microsoft's named Volkswagen story](https://www.microsoft.com/en/customers/story/24120-volkswagen-microsoft-copilot) describes Volkswagen use of Codebeamer Copilot and requirement/test authoring work. | It discloses no accuracy, evaluation population, or study method. The 20–40% time-saving statement is attributed to general Codebeamer customer author/review/validate workflows; it is not a Volkswagen AI benefit measurement. |
| 18 Aug 2025 | [PTC Codebeamer 3.1 release post](https://community.ptc.com/codebeamer-445/codebeamer-3-1-has-released-168030) identifies requirement and test assistants as Applied AI beta/private preview. | General AI availability at that date. |
| 19 Dec 2025 | PTC Community announces [Codebeamer 3.2](https://community.ptc.com/codebeamer-445/codebeamer-3-2-has-released-171153) and [AI 1.0](https://community.ptc.com/codebeamer-445/codebeamer-ai-1-0-has-released-171154) releases. | A quantified accuracy or ROI result for this synthetic project. |
| 15 Jan 2026 | [PTC newsroom announcement](https://www.ptc.com/en/news/2026/ptc-delivers-new-ai-functionality-with-new-alm-releases) formally announces the ALM releases and new AI functionality. | The December Community release announcement should not be relabeled as a January product-development date. |

The transferable design question is how to keep source evidence, proposal generation, review authority, and execution eligibility separate. This repository implements those mechanics for twelve fictional test definitions without a vector database.
